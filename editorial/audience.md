# Who uses MySpeedPuzzling, and why

What the owner has told us about the community's motivations. Read it before planning or drafting posts, and use it
to pick angles, hooks and the "why it matters" line. Add to it whenever the owner shares more context about the site
or its users (date + source per entry). No member names here.

## Two groups, one community

- **The world's fastest speed puzzlers.** MSP is their home: they're extremely competitive, and MSP makes it easy to
  track and analyse their times, see where they still have room to improve, and compare with others. For them it's a
  training and analysis tool, and the place where records and competitions live. (owner, 2026-10-18)
- **Everyone else, at every pace.** Slow times are welcome and wanted. MSP helps them watch their own improvement,
  stay motivated and get faster; the community is welcoming and encouraging, never shaming. (owner, 2026-10-18)
- Both belong in the same post: celebrate the fastest, invite everyone, and frame speed as something to admire and
  aim for, never as a yardstick against the reader. See the matching rule in `standing-feedback.md`. (owner, 2026-10-18)

## Motivations worth using in posts

- The world's fastest solo 500-piece time (16:30 as of Oct 2026) is logged on MSP: the fastest of the fastest are
  here. Check the current value with `queries/about-fastest-500.sql` before using it. (owner, 2026-10-18)
- Track every time in one place (stopwatch or typed in; solo, pair, team). (owner, 2026-10-18)
- Analyse: where you lose time, how you improve over puzzles. Data angle: the same puzzlers' 1st → 20th new 500-piece
  solo got typically 12% faster (`queries/about-improvement.sql`, 2026-10-18). (owner + data, 2026-10-18)
- Compare: with others on the same puzzle, in rankings, at competitions. (owner, 2026-10-18)
- Competitions: online and in person, run on MSP for free by organisers; live results. (2026-10-18, site research)
