# Bake the music in, keep stickers manual

@myspeedpuzzling can publish Reels and Stories with good audio, without opening the app, by **baking a fitted, mastered music bed into the MP4 before publishing**. That is how essentially every automated Instagram pipeline does it, and Instagram's own head has said repeatedly that API-published or scheduled Reels get the same reach as ones posted in the app. Instagram's new Audio API (1 June 2026) does attach library music automatically, but **only to Reels, never to Stories, and not for apps on Instagram Login**, which is what MSP uses. Getting it would mean a second app on Meta's new "Unified Login" (a Meta business portfolio, no Facebook Page), and it would still give no start offset and no preview. The music itself can come from three places that all fit a small company: **Meta Sound Collection** (free, Instagram/Facebook only, no API), **Epidemic Sound Business** (about $360 a year, with an API that cuts any track to an exact length), or **local models on the M3 Max**. Among local models, **Stable Audio 3 medium** and **ACE-Step 1.5** are the two worth benchmarking. The matching, fitting, beat-snapping, mixing and QA steps are all light CPU work with open tools. What stays manual is narrow but real: **every Story sticker** (above all the weekly "Full report" link sticker), the app's own catalogue music on Stories, trending sounds unless Unified Login is set up, Trial Reel graduation, pinning, and the owner's approval of every post. The evidence on reach is thin: a muted Reel is the only audio choice with a documented penalty, and nothing shows that app-added music beats a good baked track for an account of MSP's size. MSP's own A/B, using the `media_audio_type` field, will be the best evidence available.

## Instagram's Audio API attaches music to Reels only, and not from our login

On 1 June 2026 Meta added the **Instagram Audio API**. An app can search royalty-free Sound Collection music and other creators' original sounds (`GET /ig_audio?audio_type=music|original_sound`), then attach one to a Reel at creation through `audio_configuration` (`audio_id`, plus `audio_volume` and `video_volume`). Leaving out `search_query` returns trending audio ([Meta: Audio API](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing/audio-api); [Meta changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/changelog)). The documentation states the limit plainly: **"Not supported on the Instagram API with Instagram Login."** Third-party integrators confirm it: Ayrshare rejects Instagram-Login accounts with its own error code 514 ("Audio API requires Facebook Login"), and bundle.social returns 400 for them ([Ayrshare](https://www.ayrshare.com/docs/apis/post/social-networks/instagram); [bundle.social](https://info.bundle.social/api-reference/platforms/instagram-music)). No changelog entry through November extends it to Instagram Login. A September change reported by a third-party tracker (not confirmed on Meta's own page) makes graph.instagram.com answer Facebook-Login-only parameters with a 400 error, so MSP's current app can't simply send the field and hope ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/meta/instagram-platform)).

Even where it works, the feature is narrow. Publishing with audio is documented **only for `media_type=REELS`**. Stories, carousels and images are excluded, and Ayrshare rejects them with code 515 ([Ayrshare](https://www.ayrshare.com/docs/apis/post/social-networks/instagram)). The catalogue is limited to tracks "authorized for third party use" and "may differ from the native app". **There is no preview** and **no start-offset parameter**, so a track presumably plays from 0 s, and the song's hook can't be chosen ([Meta: Audio API](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing/audio-api)). A Czech developer hit exactly that wall in October, gave up on the API and cut the audio into the MP4 himself ([GitHub g-gallery #104](https://github.com/pavelprokes/g-gallery/pull/104)). Two side effects matter for MSP's tooling. Since 30 July, `media_url` is omitted for any video carrying licensed or library audio, "even for the app user's own media", so the insights puller would have to fall back to `permalink` or `thumbnail_url` ([Meta changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/changelog)). And nobody has published how the song label renders on an API Reel, or what happens when Trial Reel `trial_params` and `audio_configuration` are combined. Metricool says Trial Reels with audio work in its scheduler, which suggests the combination is accepted ([Metricool help](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-add-audio-to-your-instagram-reels-from-metricool-2o2ks)).

The cheaper way in is no longer "link a Facebook Page". Meta now runs a third setup, the **Instagram API with Unified Login**, and recommends it for "new integrations". It needs no Facebook Page, but the app must be **owned by a Meta business portfolio**. It runs on graph.facebook.com with `instagram_*` rather than `instagram_business_*` permissions, and no retirement date for Instagram Login has been announced ([Meta: Platform overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/overview); [Meta: Unified Login get started](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-unified-login/get-started); [Meta: Unified Login migration](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-unified-login/migration)). The Audio API page lists Unified Login as supported, yet the same page also asks for "a Facebook Page connected to that account" and a User token. That is a contradiction inside Meta's own documentation, so the first live call is the real test. Besides API music, a switch would bring Story `link_clicks`, saves, shares and reposts counts, and resumable uploads (all Facebook Login only), and, per developer reports, tokens that never expire ([Meta changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/changelog); [postiz PR #2404](https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app/pull/2404)). Standard Access "is enough to test with people who have a role on your app", so a single-owner app may avoid App Review. That is an inference to verify, not a promise. The lowest-risk shape is a **second, Unified-Login app used only for Reels with API music**, leaving the working Instagram-Login publisher untouched, because one app reportedly can't serve both login types ([CRPRO Hub](https://crprohub.com/en/blog/instagram-api-instagram-login-facebook-login-unified-login)).

## Automated publishers bake the audio, and reach doesn't punish them for it

Commercial schedulers show where the line runs. **Metricool** (since 18 May 2026), **Buffer** (Page-linked connections only), Planoly and Pallyy attach library music to scheduled Reels through the new API. Later, Publer and Blotato still auto-publish "original audio only" and tell users to bake the music into the file or fall back to a phone notification ([Metricool help](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-add-audio-to-your-instagram-reels-from-metricool-2o2ks); [Buffer](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/using-instagram-with-buffer-YSjg2dXFV8); [Later](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/5023195813143-Schedule-Publish-Instagram-Reels); [Blotato](https://www.blotato.com/blog/best-instagram-post-schedulers)). For Stories every tool gives the same answer: **no music, no link, poll or location stickers through any API**. A Story that needs them becomes "notification publishing": the phone buzzes, and a human finishes the post in the app ([Planable](https://help.planable.io/hc/en-us/articles/21715310379804-Understanding-what-API-is-and-how-it-affects-your-scheduling-in-Planable); [Meta: IG User Media](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media)). The fully automatic pipelines on n8n and GitHub never touch the Audio API. They mux a bed into the MP4 before calling the Graph API: ElevenLabs or Kokoro voiceovers, Pixabay or bundled royalty-free tracks at about 20 % under the voice, or Google Lyria scores at about $0.08 a track ([OpenReels](https://github.com/tsensei/OpenReels); [ReelsKit-Engine](https://github.com/FassihShah/ReelsKit-Engine); [n8n #3442](https://n8n.io/workflows/3442-fully-automated-ai-video-generation-and-multi-platform-publishing/)). MSP's model of rendering locally, baking the audio and publishing through the API is the community's standard design. The library-music step is the one part almost nobody automates.

On reach, the strongest statement is about the publishing route, not the music. Adam Mosseri has said in March 2025, March 2026 and August 2026 that scheduled or third-party publishing does not change reach: "You can schedule a Reel, or you can post it yourself." These quotes come via vendor blogs, not original video links ([Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/blog/does-scheduling-instagram-posts-hurt-reach/)). Ranking is driven by **watch time, likes and sends** ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/)). Instagram's ranking explainer lists the audio track as a ranking input and "go to the audio page" as a predicted action, and names **muted Reels** among the content it shows less. Silence is therefore the one audio choice with a documented cost ([Instagram Ranking Explained, 2023](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)). The popular "+29 % reach for trending audio" figure compares against Reels *without any audio* and cites no study ([Adobe Express](https://adobe.com/express/learn/blog/trending-audio-social-media-content)). The only test with a stated method, one agency's own Reels, found **no significant difference** between original audio and named library music (reach p = 0.87, engagement p = 0.14), with an unreported sample size ([That Random Agency](https://www.thatrandomagency.com/blog/do-trending-sounds-impact-instagram-reel-performance)). For Stories there is no credible evidence that a music sticker raises reach, and one source notes that adding the sticker forces another compression pass ([Socialz.ai](https://socialz.ai/blog/how-to-add-music-to-instagram-story); [EzUGC](https://www.ezugc.ai/blog/instagram-story-blurry-when-i-add-music)).

The honest reading for MSP is that **library music buys one extra discovery channel**: the Reel appears on the sound's audio page. Its value is unknown and probably small for non-trend data Reels on a niche account. MSP's own numbers point to the same conclusion: the Friday-13th Reel had music added in the app and still reached 631, 0.82× the account's Reel median of 766. Its viewers watched (48 % skip rate) but didn't send it, so hook and topic dominated, not the soundtrack ([STATUS](../docs/STATUS.md)). The new `media_audio_type` field (`MUSIC` or `ORIGINAL_SOUND`) lets the insights puller label each Reel and compare baked against app-added audio against the format median. The sample will be small, though: a handful of Reels per arm can only detect very large differences.

## Three bed sources fit a small company: Meta's collection, Epidemic, or the Mac itself

Instagram's in-app catalogue is the wrong benchmark: business accounts get a restricted set, and the main library is licensed for personal, non-commercial use ([Audiodrome](https://audiodrome.net/for-creators/instagram-music-library/)). For baked beds, three routes are clean, and the rest have a catch.

| Source | Licence fit for MSP | Cost | Automation | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta Sound Collection** | Commercial use, **only in content on Meta platforms** | Free | None: manual MP3 download, tag locally | Lowest claim risk on Instagram; unusable if a video is cross-posted to YouTube or TikTok |
| **Epidemic Sound Business** | Brands under $10M revenue; content published while subscribed stays cleared forever | $30/mo billed yearly (~$360/yr), $75 month to month | **Best**: Connect API and MCP server, mood/BPM search, `POST /v0/tracks/versions` cuts to any length from 1 s to 5 min, `highlights` endpoint, stems | Account must be **safelisted**; anything posted after cancelling gets claimed |
| **Local models** (below) | MIT (ACE-Step) or Stability Community Licence (under $1M revenue) | Electricity | Full | AI-music sentiment; resemblance to existing works |
| Uppbeat Business | Required for companies since 2 Sep 2026 | ~$35/mo billed yearly | No API | Manual downloads |
| Pixabay / Mixkit / Freesound CC0 | Commercial OK | Free | No music API; local catalogue | **Pixabay shield-icon tracks are Content-ID registered** and can be muted |
| Jamendo, Soundstripe, Musicbed, Artlist | Paid licences | €29/track up to quote-only API | Jamendo API is non-commercial; Soundstripe API is enterprise | Over-priced or manual for one account |

Sources: [Lilach Bullock on Sound Collection](https://www.lilachbullock.com/meta-sound-collection/); [Activepieces on Epidemic pricing](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/epidemic-sound-pricing-plans-and-costs-2026-guide); [Epidemic cancellation](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/25890380656402-Cancellation); [Epidemic Create versions](https://developers.epidemicsound.com/docs/create-versions/); [Epidemic MCP](https://developers.epidemicsound.com/docs/mcp/); [Uppbeat pricing](https://uppbeat.io/pricing); [Pixabay FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/); [Jamendo pricing](https://licensing.jamendo.com/en/pricing); [Soundstripe API](https://www.soundstripe.com/api).

Epidemic is the only library whose API does the hard part (search by mood, then cut to the exact Reel length while keeping the structure), and its MCP server can even pin a section at a time offset, for example the chorus at 0:15 ([Epidemic MCP](https://developers.epidemicsound.com/docs/mcp/)). One third party claims API keys have been self-serve on the Business plan since October. That isn't confirmed, and it needs checking before paying. Sound Collection is the zero-cost alternative. It matches what the Audio API would offer anyway (Meta's "royalty-free Sound Collection music"), so **baking a Sound Collection track gets most of the API's catalogue without the login switch**. It loses only the song label and the audio-page link.

Local generation has moved on since October. **Stable Audio 3** (20 May 2026) supersedes Stable Audio Open. Its open small-music (459M) and medium (1.4B) weights were trained only on AudioSparx-licensed and Freesound CC audio, and the paper's instrumental benchmark puts medium well ahead of ACE-Step 1.5 XL-turbo (overall quality 4.20 vs 3.35, FAD 0.107 vs 0.193). It needs **5.5 GB at 30 s**, and small-music renders 30 s in 0.63 s on an M4 via CoreML ([arXiv 2605.17991](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17991v1)). Its catches: weights are gated behind a click-through, commercial use requires registering with Stability, and the licence wants "Powered by Stability AI" shown when distributing. Whether that applies to posted outputs is ambiguous, and a line on the website settles it cheaply ([Stability Community License](https://stability.ai/community-license-agreement)). The paper also warns that 20 s clips score worse because short training samples were mostly loops. **ACE-Step 1.5** (MIT weights, including XL) has the richest controls: BPM, key, time signature, duration from 10 s, repaint, cover and stems. It has an official MLX path, and one measured Mac figure: **about 16 s per 30 s clip at 9.5 GB on an M5** with xl-turbo ([HF model card](https://huggingface.co/ACE-Step/Ace-Step1.5); [GitHub as15](https://github.com/jameshiew/as15)). LoRA training on Apple Silicon is still an open pull request ([ACE-Step issues](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5/issues?q=mac+OR+mps+OR+mlx)). ComfyUI, already installed here, supports both natively ([ComfyUI blog](https://blog.comfy.org/p/stable-audio-3-day-0-support)). **Magenta RealTime 2** (CC-BY-4.0, native on Apple Silicon, steerable by MIDI) is the interesting outsider for variations on an MSP signature motif ([Magenta blog](https://magenta.withgoogle.com/magenta-realtime-2)). MiniMax Music 3 needs CUDA and carries a mandatory AI-disclosure clause, and YuE2 and MusicGen are non-commercial and older; none earns a slot in the benchmark ([MiniMax LICENSE](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-Music3/blob/main/LICENSE)). Both shortlisted models are commercially licensed, so the owner's tolerance for non-commercial licences never comes into play.

The policy risk is manageable rather than zero. Meta's mandatory label covers "photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio", and AI instrumental music is "not listed", so an unobtrusive bed falls outside it, consistent with the owner's no-disclosure decision ([Postora](https://www.postora.org/blog/ai-generated-content-labels-social-media-2026)). Mosseri warned on 29 Oct that the AI backlash "is only going to get worse", strongest among creative communities ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91632443/instagram-head-adam-mosseri-warns-creators-that-ai-backlash-is-only-going-to-get-worse)). Puzzlers are not a music scene, but beds should stay in the background. Prompts should never name artists, and each bed's model, version, prompt and seed should be archived as evidence in case of a claim.

## Matching, fitting and mixing are a solved, light CPU problem

Since MSP renders video from code, the right order is **fit the music first, then move the animation to the music**. Moving the music would mean refitting; shifting a reveal by up to a quarter second is invisible. The step chain uses commercially safe tools only.

**Select.** The draft writes a one-line brief ("cozy, playful, ticking clock, light piano, 100–120 BPM"). A local `library.json` holds each track's source, licence, BPM, instrumental flag, hand tags and ending type. Tracks are ranked by **CLAP** text-to-audio similarity (Microsoft CLAP, MIT code; LAION-CLAP, CC0 code) after hard filters (instrumental, BPM window, long enough, not used recently), and the owner hears the **top three**, not one auto-pick ([microsoft/CLAP](https://github.com/microsoft/CLAP); [LAION-AI/CLAP](https://github.com/LAION-AI/CLAP)). CLAP's zero-shot genre accuracy on music is only 51–71 %, so it ranks rather than decides. The stronger music taggers (Essentia's mood heads, MuQ-MuLan) carry non-commercial weights ([Essentia models](https://essentia.upf.edu/models.html)). This is the same pattern Epidemic documents for its own "LLM brief → semantic search → pick" flow ([Epidemic soundtracking](https://developers.epidemicsound.com/docs/soundtracking-with-llm/)).

**Fit.** For 8–30 s videos the job is picking a window of whole bars and ending it well, not extending. **musicfit** (MIT, plain Node, ffmpeg only) does almost exactly that. It finds beats and bars, scores one-bar slots by dynamic programming with 30 ms crossfades just before downbeats, keeps the track's own final hit if it fits (otherwise a bar-long fade on a beat), can place the drop on a chosen frame (`--hit 450f`), and exports beats and bars as frame numbers in `cues.json`. It is immature (one star, tested only on Ubuntu), so it has to pass a lab before adoption ([EminBudak/musicfit](https://github.com/EminBudak/musicfit)). The fallback is beat_this downbeats plus about 100 lines of bar-similarity DP. Rubber Band's `--fine -D` stretch can absorb a small leftover (GPL, which is fine in a private pipeline) ([Rubber Band](https://breakfastquay.com/rubberband/usage.txt)). The cheapest fix for a bar-rounding gap is to stretch the HTML timeline by a few frames.

**Align, mix, master.** Re-run beat tracking on the *fitted* file, since seams shift beats. Then snap GSAP labels to the nearest downbeat within ±0.25 s (or the nearest beat within ±0.12 s). These tolerances are design guesses, not sourced. GSAP's absolute positions and labels make this a data injection, not a rewrite ([GSAP position parameter](https://gsap.com/resources/position-parameter/)). SFX (ticks on count-ups, whooshes about 0.2 s before cuts) come from the same timeline JSON through `adelay` and `amix=normalize=0`. With code-known events, plain volume dips of 3–6 dB are more predictable than a sidechain. A high-pass at 80–120 Hz suits phone speakers, and the existing two-pass `loudnorm` to −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP finishes the master ([ffmpeg filters](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html)).

**QA.** An automated report per render checks integrated loudness and true peak (`ebur128`), clipping (`astats`), gaps (`silencedetect`), tail energy for abrupt endings, audio length equal to video length to the frame, and musicfit's per-seam `likeness` scores, which show where to listen first. After publishing, IG Media's `copyright_check_information.status` can tell the +24 h check whether a Reel was claimed or muted ([IG Media reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/reference/instagram-media)). None of this is a heavy job: seconds per clip on CPU. Only generating beds with ComfyUI or MLX falls under the owner's go-ahead rule.

Sources: [Meta: IG User Media](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media); [Ayrshare](https://www.ayrshare.com/docs/apis/post/social-networks/instagram); [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/meta/instagram-platform).

For MSP this reshapes the manual work rather than removing it. A Reel can go out fully through the API, once Reels publishing is built (it isn't yet), with a baked bed and `audio_name` set to something like "MySpeedPuzzling – original audio". A Story that needs no sticker can go out fully automatically too. A Story that carries a link sticker still needs the owner's thumb, so the realistic gain there is that **he adds one sticker instead of a sticker plus a music search**. All Story text stays in the frame, since the caption field didn't show on 13 Nov.

## Recommended path and a small test plan

The path has four steps, in order. **First**, build API Reels publishing with baked audio (container `media_type=REELS`, `cover_url`, optional `trial_params`, ffprobe preflight). Make baked audio the default for Reels and video Stories, so the app is needed only for stickers. **Second**, build the `music-bed` step (select → fit → align → mix → master → QA) on a local, licence-ledgered library seeded with 30–50 Meta Sound Collection tracks, at zero cost. **Third**, benchmark the local models and, in parallel, run a one-month Epidemic Business trial against the same briefs. Keep whichever wins the owner's blind listening. **Fourth**, decide on the Unified-Login app later, and only if MSP's own A/B shows app-added music beating baked beds. It is a business-portfolio setup and a second app for one discovery channel of unproven value.

The benchmark is one quiet-hours lab. It needs the owner's go-ahead, a calmed machine, and the usual records: wall time, peak memory and swap, GPU busy time, per-item time, and what else was running.

| Test | Candidates | Set | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local bed generation | **Stable Audio 3 medium** (ComfyUI and mlx-community), **SA3 small-music**, **ACE-Step 1.5 XL-turbo** and **2B turbo** (MLX, no LM planner); optional **Magenta RT 2** from an MSP motif | 5 MSP moods (playful, cozy, ticking-clock tension, lo-fi, celebratory) × 3 seeds × 15 s and 30 s, i.e. 30 per model | Seconds per 30 s bed, peak memory/swap, BPM adherence (beat_this measured vs requested), clean-ending rate after fitting, QA pass rate, owner verdicts Works / Unsure / No-go per bed |
| Library baseline | Meta Sound Collection (free), Epidemic Business (`versions` API to exact length) | Same 5 briefs, top-3 CLAP picks each | Same owner verdicts, played **blind** alongside the generated beds in the lab gallery |
| Fit and mix | musicfit on macOS vs in-house beat_this DP | 10 tracks fitted to 3 real MSP timelines (weekly Story 15 s, Unlucky-style 30 s, a 42 s loop) | Seam likeness, audible-seam marks by the owner, frames moved by snapping, run time |
| Reach (real posts, no test posts) | Baked bed vs app-added music | Alternate on the next 6–8 follower Reels, logged by `media_audio_type` | Reach, skip rate, average watch, sends vs the Reel median. Read as directional only at this n |

The owner has six decisions to make. Each is listed with a recommendation first:

| # | Decision | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bake audio by default and stop adding music in the app? | Yes for Reels and video Stories; keep app music as an exception for a trend-driven Reel |
| 2 | Go-ahead for the local-model benchmark night (ComfyUI/MLX), plus accepting Stability's gated weights and Community Licence registration | Yes, one night, on a calmed Mac |
| 3 | Pay for one month of Epidemic Business ($75 month to month) as the library baseline? | Yes, but only after confirming the API key comes with the plan; otherwise use Sound Collection alone |
| 4 | Create a Meta business portfolio and a second, Unified-Login app for API music on Reels? | Not now; revisit after the reach A/B |
| 5 | AI-music stance: keep "no disclosure", archive prompt, seed and model per bed, add "Powered by Stability AI" on the website if SA3 wins | Yes, consistent with the existing AI decisions |
| 6 | The A/B runs on real, approved Reels (no test posts on the main account): accept alternating audio types for 6–8 Reels? | Yes, with the owner able to override for any single Reel |

## Conclusion

The question changed shape during the research. In October, "API music" looked like the prize and Instagram Login like the obstacle. In fact the API catalogue is essentially Meta Sound Collection plus original sounds, without a start offset, a preview or Story support, and that same collection can be downloaded and baked in today. What separates app-added and baked music is not sound quality but an audio-page link of unknown worth, and the pipeline that bakes audio is also the one that can snap every reveal to a downbeat, which Instagram's music picker can't do. The real bottleneck for hands-free posting was never music. It is the **Story link sticker**, which no API anywhere offers. The best automation target is therefore Reels and sticker-free Stories end to end, with the owner's manual time shrinking to one sticker on the weekly Story.

Two uncertainties deserve watching rather than acting on: whether Meta extends the Audio API, or Story stickers, to Unified or Instagram Login in 2027, and whether the AI-music backlash reaches hobby communities. Both argue for a pipeline whose bed source is a swappable entry in `library.json`, with the licence ledger the project already keeps.
