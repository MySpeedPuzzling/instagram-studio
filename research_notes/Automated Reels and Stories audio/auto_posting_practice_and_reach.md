# Automated Reels and Stories audio: how tools and creators handle it, and what is known about reach

Researched 16 Nov 2026. Scope: 2025 to Nov 2026; older evidence is labelled. "Vendor" means a company selling a scheduler or a music/analytics tool, so it has an interest in the answer.

## Q1. Schedulers: can they auto-publish Reels and Stories, and can they attach Instagram library music?

### Takeaway
Since mid-2026 Meta has an **Instagram Audio API** that lets third-party apps attach Instagram library music or other creators' original sounds to **Reels only**, and **only for apps using Facebook Login** (Business/Creator account linked to a Facebook Page). With **Instagram Login** (which MSP uses) there is still no way to attach library music through the API. **Stories cannot get music or stickers through the API at all**, so every scheduler falls back to "notification/mobile publishing" for Stories with music. Metricool, Buffer (Facebook connection only), Planoly and Pallyy use the new API for Reels; Later, Publer and Blotato still say "original audio only" for auto-publishing.

### Cited Findings
**Meta platform (primary)**
- 1 June 2026 changelog: "Introducing the Instagram Audio API for apps using Facebook Login." Apps can search/retrieve original sounds from Reels and royalty-free Sound Collection music and attach them to Reels at creation. Endpoints `GET /ig_audio?audio_type={music|original_sound}`, `GET /{ig_audio_id}`, plus an ads endpoint for copyright-replacement audio. Instagram Login is not mentioned. — [Meta Instagram Platform changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/changelog.md)
- 1 June 2026: new `media_audio_type` field on IG Media returns `MUSIC` or `ORIGINAL_SOUND` ("applies to all versions"). Useful to log which audio type each of our Reels ended up with. — [Meta changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/changelog.md)
- 30 July 2026: `media_url` is omitted for video media (Reels and video Stories) containing copyrighted/licensed audio, "including audio added from the Instagram audio library"; applies to both graph.facebook.com and graph.instagram.com. — [Meta changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/changelog.md)
- 3 Dec 2025: Trial Reels supported in the Content Publishing API, for both Facebook Login and Instagram Login (`trial_params`). — [Meta changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/changelog.md)
- `audio_name` on a Reels container only renames the audio already in the video (once); it does not add music. The IG User Media reference still says story "Publishing stickers (i.e., link, poll, location) is not supported". — [Meta IG User Media reference](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media); summary in [search result on Stories limits, incl. Ayrshare/Planable](https://help.planable.io/hc/en-us/articles/21715310379804-Understanding-what-API-is-and-how-it-affects-your-scheduling-in-Planable)
- A third-party blog dates the opening of the audio library to "May 2026"; Metricool says its feature launched 18 May 2026, i.e. some partners had access before the 1 June changelog entry. — [noobclaw blog](https://noobclaw.com/blog/schedule-reels-trending-audio-api-2026/); [Metricool help](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-add-audio-to-your-instagram-reels-from-metricool-2o2ks)

**API middlemen (developer docs)**
- Ayrshare: `audioConfiguration` (`audioId`, `audioVolume` 0–100, `videoVolume` 0–100) on Reels only; Stories/images/carousels rejected with code 515; Instagram Login rejected with code 514 ("Audio API requires Facebook Login"); code 517 = track invalid, region-restricted or "not licensed for this use". — [Ayrshare Instagram docs](https://www.ayrshare.com/docs/apis/post/social-networks/instagram)
- bundle.social (Nov 2026): `musicSoundInfo.musicSoundId` with separate volumes; music only for Facebook-Login-connected accounts. — [bundle.social](https://bundle.social/instagram-api)
- Older guides that say the API cannot add library music (e.g. an April 2026 GitHub reference, postproxy, TokPortal) predate the Audio API or assume Instagram Login; they remain correct for Instagram Login. — [GitHub gist reference, Apr 2026](https://gist.github.com/jameschapman2c/65eff9f54a2d350b17a6ce5127b9fe42); [postproxy](https://postproxy.dev/blog/instagram-reels-api-publishing-guide/); [TokPortal](https://www.tokportal.com/vs/instagram-graph-api-native-reels-posting-alternative)

**Per tool (help centres unless noted)**
- **Metricool**: "Scheduling Reels with audio is available as of May 18, 2026"; music from Meta's library or other creators' original sounds; works for regular and Trial Reels; requires connecting via Facebook; shows only tracks Instagram allows for scheduling tools ("some songs you see in the app won't be there"); Reels with unavailable audio or interactive stickers go to notification publishing; Feed posts and Stories do not support audio scheduling. — [Metricool help](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-add-audio-to-your-instagram-reels-from-metricool-2o2ks); [Metricool May 2026 product updates](https://metricool.com/product-updates-may-2026/)
- **Buffer**: newer pages: with a Facebook-Page-linked connection you can search Instagram's audio library and add music to a Reel that still auto-publishes; other connection methods cannot. Music on a Post or Story, or any sticker, switches the post to "Notify Me". Older Buffer pages (≈100–200 days old) still say music always needs notification publishing, so Buffer's help centre is internally inconsistent. — [Buffer: Using Instagram with Buffer](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/using-instagram-with-buffer-YSjg2dXFV8); [Buffer: connecting Instagram](https://support.buffer.com/articles/connecting-your-instagram-account-to-buffer-n9Ad6veXsu); [Buffer: music, stickers and effects](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/adding-music-stickers-and-other-effects-to-instagram-and-tiktok-posts-G5QrWfQL4k). Blotato's 5 Nov 2026 comparison still lists Buffer as "No" for library music — conflicting with Buffer's newer page. — [Blotato comparison](https://www.blotato.com/blog/best-instagram-post-schedulers)
- **Later**: "Auto Published Reels support original audio only"; auto publish doesn't support "audio from Instagram's music or audio library"; for music, use notification publishing and add the sound in Instagram; Reels guide updated 21 Apr 2026 says the same. Later's community advice: bake the audio into the file before upload if you want auto-publish. — [Later: Schedule & Publish Reels](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/5023195813143-Schedule-Publish-Instagram-Reels); [Later: Publishing Restrictions](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/36919457087639-Instagram-Publishing-Restrictions-in-Later); [Later: Notification Publishing](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/41345601472535-Publish-Instagram-Posts-Using-Notification-Publishing); [Later community](https://community.later.com/t5/Using-Later/Using-Later-to-add-Music-to-Instagram-Reels/m-p/9832?nobounce)
- **Publer**: Reel "must already include music or audio before uploading" (help article ≈49 days old); otherwise use reminder/push publishing. Feature request #1363 "Schedule Reels with a trending sound" was "On Hold"; a later update notes Meta's new Audio API, but no sign Publer has shipped it. — [Publer: schedule Reels](https://publer.com/help/en/article/how-to-schedule-instagram-reels-1a7l0mu/); [Publer: trending audio](https://publer.com/help/en/article/how-to-add-trending-audio-to-instagram-reels-9vehbt/); [Publer feedback #1363](https://feedback.publer.com/1363)
- **Planoly, Pallyy**: attach library songs to scheduled Reels via a Facebook-linked connection; Pallyy Stories cannot include polls, music or stickers (per Blotato, 5 Nov 2026; a competitor's article, not their own help pages). An older Framekit comparison (≈Aug 2026) listed Planoly as notification-only for trending audio, so this changed recently or one source is wrong. — [Blotato comparison](https://www.blotato.com/blog/best-instagram-post-schedulers); [Framekit comparison](https://framekit.ai/blog/best-social-media-schedulers-for-creatives-2026)
- **Blotato** (an AI auto-posting tool): "Blotato can't attach a song from Instagram's library" — original audio only. — [Blotato comparison](https://www.blotato.com/blog/best-instagram-post-schedulers)
- **Hootsuite**: direct publishing "only available for Instagram Business profiles"; Stories and Creator profiles go to notification publishing; library music not documented. — [Blotato comparison](https://www.blotato.com/blog/best-instagram-post-schedulers)
- **Meta Business Suite** (desktop): can create and schedule a Reel and add music from an "Audio" tab with separate volume for original audio and music; guides say the library is limited to music licensed for business use; guides disagree on whether trending audio is offered. Full catalogue only in the Instagram app (which can also schedule Reels natively up to 75 days ahead). — [graphed.com guide](https://www.graphed.com/blog/how-to-schedule-reels-on-instagram-meta-business-suite); [Sprout Social: schedule Reels](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/schedule-instagram-reels/); [PlugKit](https://www.plugkit.co/blog/can-you-schedule-instagram-stories-music-reels/)
- **Stories via API (all tools)**: no stickers, no music, no polls/links/location; video Stories 3–60 s; schedulers use reminder publishing for Stories with music or stickers. — [Ayrshare](https://www.ayrshare.com/docs/apis/post/social-networks/instagram); [Planable help](https://help.planable.io/hc/en-us/articles/21715310379804-Understanding-what-API-is-and-how-it-affects-your-scheduling-in-Planable); [tryadeli](https://www.tryadeli.com/blog/instagram-stories-api)

### Inferences
- For MSP (Instagram Login, no Facebook Page link assumed) the only fully automatic Reel option today is baked-in audio (shows up as "Original audio" / `ORIGINAL_SOUND`). Getting library music automatically would require re-connecting via Facebook Login with a linked Page, and even then only a subset of tracks is licensed for API use, and only for Reels.
- Stories with library music cannot be automated by anyone; the industry-standard answer is a phone notification and finishing in the app.
- The "business account" library restriction applies in the app too, so a Business account's in-app choice may also be narrower than a personal creator's.

### Gaps
- Sprout Social's and Hootsuite's own 2026 help pages on Audio API support were not found; status unknown.
- Whether Meta plans the Audio API for Instagram Login apps: no statement found.
- Which share of trending tracks is licensed for API use: no numbers found (Metricool only says "some" are missing).

## Q2. Fully automatic pipelines (n8n / Make / Zapier / GitHub): what do they do for audio?

### Takeaway
Fully automated Reel pipelines almost always **bake the audio into the MP4** before posting via the Graph API: TTS voiceover (mostly ElevenLabs, also Kokoro), royalty-free stock tracks (Pixabay, bundled libraries), or AI music (Google Lyria, Mubert-type APIs). None found uses the Instagram Audio API, and none uses Suno in a documented way.

### Cited Findings
- n8n template #3442: ElevenLabs voiceover, Creatomate assembly, upload-post.com publishes to TikTok/Instagram/YouTube etc. — [n8n #3442](https://n8n.io/workflows/3442-fully-automated-ai-video-generation-and-multi-platform-publishing/)
- n8n template #5100: GPT-4o + RunwayML + ElevenLabs 20 s faceless videos; posting still "Coming Soon". — [n8n #5100](https://n8n.io/workflows/5100-gpt-4o-runwayml-elevenlabs-for-social-media/)
- Medium tutorial (n8n): idea → image-to-video 10 s loop → "generates background music that fits the scene and muxes it to the video" → posts via Graph API. — [Medium: From Idea to Instagram Reel](https://gowtamsingulur.medium.com/from-idea-to-instagram-reel-fully-automated-with-n8n-image-video-music-post-92f69728ab5b)
- mTylewski/Instagram_reels_n8n_workflow: Gemini + ElevenLabs + Creatomate, posts on schedule via Facebook Graph API. — [GitHub](https://github.com/mTylewski/Instagram_reels_n8n_workflow)
- IlyassCODEX/Faceless-Reels-AI-Agent: n8n, Groq, Flux Schnell, FFmpeg, Cloudinary for public URL, then publish. — [GitHub](https://github.com/IlyassCODEX/Faceless-Reels-AI-Agent-for-Instagram)
- tsensei/OpenReels: `--music-provider (bundled, lyria)`: Google Lyria 3 Pro AI score (~$0.08/track) "scene-synced to the emotional arc", or 25 bundled royalty-free tracks; renders only, no posting. — [GitHub](https://github.com/tsensei/OpenReels)
- FassihShah/ReelsKit-Engine: mood-based Pixabay royalty-free music at 20 % volume under voiceover. — [GitHub](https://github.com/FassihShah/ReelsKit-Engine)
- aiventajsf-cmd/makeasy-reels: Kokoro TTS + optional royalty-free MP3s; GitHub Pages as the public URL, Make webhook posts. — [GitHub](https://github.com/aiventajsf-cmd/makeasy-reels)
- ruud0/ig-autoposter: music producers' beats + FFmpeg waveform video, Claude captions, Graph API posting (audio = the creator's own track). — [GitHub](https://github.com/ruud0/ig-autoposter)
- Creatomate: Zapier has no native Instagram video posting, so Zapier flows need a scheduler (e.g. SocialBee); n8n flows call the Graph API directly. — [Creatomate guide](https://creatomate.com/how-to/automate-faceless-instagram-reels)

### Inferences
- MSP's current model (render locally, bake or not bake audio, publish via API) is exactly what the automation community does; the "library music" step is the one part nobody automates.

### Gaps
- No usage statistics on how many automated accounts use voiceover vs music vs silence.
- No documented Suno-based pipeline (Suno has no widely documented official API); local models (MusicGen/Stable Audio) not found in Reel-posting repos.

## Q3. Evidence on reach: Instagram statements, data studies, silent / sound-design audio, API vs app

### Takeaway
There is **no official statement that original audio or library/trending music ranks better**, and **Mosseri has said repeatedly (Mar 2025, Mar 2026, Aug 2026) that scheduled/third-party-published posts and Reels get the same reach** as app-published ones. Instagram's own 2023 ranking explainer lists the audio track as a ranking input, "go to the audio page" as a predicted action, and **muted Reels** as deprioritised — so silence is the one audio choice with a documented penalty. The data comparing trending vs original audio is weak: the only study with a stated method (one agency account, t-tests) found **no significant difference**; the popular "+29 % reach for trending audio" figure has no traceable primary source.

### Cited Findings
**Instagram / Mosseri (primary or widely reproduced)**
- Mosseri, 22 Jan 2025: "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends"; likes weigh more for connected, sends for unconnected content. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/); [Dataslayer summary](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)
- Mosseri, 29 Mar 2025 Stories Q&A: "No, if you use something like scheduled posts, it will not affect your reach one way or the other." Repeated 19 Mar 2026; 28 Aug 2026 on Reels: "There's no difference in terms of your reach. You can schedule a Reel, or you can post it yourself." (Quotes reproduced by Kontentino, a scheduler vendor, without links to the originals.) — [Kontentino, 19 Oct 2026](https://www.kontentino.com/blog/does-scheduling-instagram-posts-hurt-reach/); also [Cadenus](https://cadenus.io/resources/blog/do-scheduling-tools-hurt-your-reach/), [Metricool](https://metricool.com/does-scheduling-social-media-posts-affect-reach/)
- Instagram Ranking Explained (Mosseri, 31 May 2023 — older): Reels ranking uses "information about the reel, such as the audio track or visuals in the video, as well as popularity"; predicted actions include "go to the audio page (a proxy for whether or not you might be inspired to make your own reel)"; Instagram makes less visible "low-resolution or watermarked reels", and the page also lists **muted reels**, reels with borders, majority-text reels, and reels already posted on Instagram. — [about.instagram.com](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- Originality policy (April 2024 — older): new ranking input for smaller creators, original content replaces identical reposts "based on audio and visual signals", aggregators removed from recommendations. Extended April 2026 to photos/carousels with a rolling 30-day "mostly unoriginal" account test. This is about reposting others' content, not about choosing music. — [iMusician on the 2024 update](https://imusician.pro/en/resources/blog/instagram-is-changing-its-algorithm-focusing-on-more-original-content); [Orphiq on April 2026 policy](https://orphiq.com/resources/instagram-algorithm-music-artists); [Lia Haberman ICYMI](https://liahaberman.substack.com/p/icymi-aprils-biggest-headlines)
- A "Mosseri checklist" including "use original audio" circulates, but only via secondary sources (Stackinfluence), not traceable to an Instagram post. Third-party blogs (Highstyle, TrueFuture, Dataslayer) claim "original audio is favoured" or "must include audio (music or voiceover)" without primary sources. — [Stackinfluence](https://stackinfluence.com/instagram-2025-algorithm-shake-up/); [Highstyle](https://www.highstyle.ai/insights/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026); [TrueFuture](https://www.truefuturemedia.com/articles/instagram-reels-reach-2026-business-growth-guide)
- Mosseri, April 2026 (as quoted by Kontentino): hack-type tactics "sometimes work, usually don't, and when we find them, we usually shut them down." Also Dec 2025 year-end memo emphasising raw, human content over AI-generated material (secondary summary). — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/blog/does-scheduling-instagram-posts-hurt-reach/); [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)

**Data studies (quality noted)**
- That Random Agency "Random Labs" (post dated 12 Mar 2025): own account's Reels from the last 6 months; original audio vs named library music; reach 142.87 vs 149.67 (p = 0.87), engagement 8.00 vs 10.08 (p = 0.14): **no significant difference**. Sample size not reported, one small account, no controls; the table also shows "<0.05" contradicting its own p-values. Weak but the only one with a stated method. — [That Random Agency](https://www.thatrandomagency.com/blog/do-trending-sounds-impact-instagram-reel-performance)
- Hootsuite experiment (2021 — older): 6 Reels; 3 with trending tracks got 17.3K/16.3K views vs 7,337/6,272 for original audio. n = 6, anecdotal. — [Hootsuite blog](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-reels-experiment/)
- "Trending audio = +29 % reach / +42 % engagement" (Adobe) — compared with Reels **without audio**, no study cited; socialmon.ai's "29–33 % above original-only" and "79 % of viral Reels used trending audio" cite unnamed "2026 analyses". Untraceable vendor claims. — [Adobe Express](https://adobe.com/express/learn/blog/trending-audio-social-media-content); [socialmon.ai](https://www.socialmon.ai/blog/viral-instagram-songs-how-to-find-trending-sounds-before-they-peak)
- IShort (vendor): "of 150 top-performing Reels, 78 % used original audio, ~21 % licensed music" — counts only winners, no reach comparison; same vendor elsewhere claims trending sounds give "20–40 % initial boost" without method. — [IShort](https://ishort.pro/original-audio-vs-trending-sounds-instagram.html)
- Reelyze (vendor): trend Reels collect ~80 % of views in the first 48–72 h then stall; reach driven first by skip rate in the first 3 s. No method. — [Reelyze](https://getreelyze.com/guides/reel-trends-vs-evergreen-content)
- Buffer (4M+ posts 2022–2024, published 2025): Reels +36 % reach vs carousels, +125 % vs single images — format only, **no audio breakdown found**. — [Buffer data](https://buffer.com/resources/buffer-data/); [Music Ally](https://musically.com/2025/07/17/buffer-reveals-its-latest-data-on-best-social-content-formats/)
- Socialinsider: ~9.8M Reels, 30–60 s Reels had the highest reach rate (5.60 %), >2 min 3.50 % (June 2026, cited second-hand); no audio finding located; Socialinsider's audio pages are monthly trending-sound lists, not studies. — [CreatorFlow citing Socialinsider](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-trends-creators-marketers/); [Socialinsider trending audio](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-trending-audio/)
- Meta-reported (ads, not organic): Reels ads with music + voiceover score +15 points on positive response vs no sound (via Marketing Week, cited by a search summary; primary not verified). — [Music Ally / Buffer data search context](https://musically.com/2025/07/17/buffer-reveals-its-latest-data-on-best-social-content-formats/) (see Gaps)
- Scheduler-vs-native tests (mostly Facebook/X, 2011–2025): mixed, small, mostly run by tool vendors; the one 2025 Instagram test (1 account, 5 posts + 5 Stories per method) found scheduled higher (8.19 % vs 6.44 % ER). — [Kontentino compilation](https://www.kontentino.com/blog/does-scheduling-instagram-posts-hurt-reach/)

**Mechanism of the "trending audio" advantage**
- Library/trending audio puts the Reel on that sound's audio page and can be surfaced to people who engaged with the sound; original audio carries the account's name on its own audio page, so others reusing it credit you. — [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm/); [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- Instagram's in-app "Trending audio" list (Professional dashboard, up to 50 tracks) is US-only per a 2026 summary. — [Socialinsider/SocialPilot search context](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-trends)

### Inferences
- Since ranking is driven by watch time, likes and sends, the audio choice matters mainly through retention; a well-fitted baked-in track or sound design is unlikely to be penalised as such. The only documented negative is a **muted** Reel — so "sound-design-only" should still contain audible audio, not silence.
- Publishing route (API vs app) should not matter per Mosseri; the difference between app-added library music and baked audio is the audio-page discovery channel, whose size for a ~2700-follower niche account is unknown and probably small for non-trend content.
- The most reliable evidence for MSP will be its own A/B: `media_audio_type` plus the existing insights pull allow comparing baked-audio Reels vs app-music Reels against the format median, though n will be small.

### Gaps
- No large, methodologically described study (Later, Buffer, Hootsuite, Socialinsider, Metricool) comparing trending vs original vs no-music Reels in 2025–2026 was found.
- No primary Instagram/Mosseri source saying original audio is ranked higher, or that library music is ranked higher.
- No data on AI-generated music specifically (only Mosseri's general AI-content remarks and the 2026 "AI info" labelling context, not verified here).
- Meta's "+15 points with music and voiceover" ads stat: primary Marketing Week/Meta source not opened.
- Original video links for the Mosseri scheduling quotes were not found; quotes rely on consistent reproduction across vendor blogs.

## Q4. Does the music sticker on Stories affect reach?

### Takeaway
No reliable evidence found. Claims of higher replies/completion (29–40 %) come from SEO/vendor blogs without method; Instagram has not said the music sticker boosts Story reach. Story reach is driven by followers who open Stories, so any effect would be indirect.

### Cited Findings
- "Instagram's algorithm does not officially confirm that music boosts Story reach"; claims of correlation with completion, replies and shares. — [Socialz.ai](https://socialz.ai/blog/how-to-add-music-to-instagram-story)
- "Stories with music stickers generate 29 % higher reply rates", attributed to an Instagram creator resource that could not be found. — [Stackinfluence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/add-music-to-instagram-post-the-creators-guide)
- Trending-audio Stories "outperform silent ones by over 30 % in retention"; ~70 % of Stories viewed with sound on — unnamed "source analysis". Same source: adding the music sticker forces another render/compression pass that can blur the Story. — [EzUGC](https://www.ezugc.ai/blog/how-to-add-music-to-instagram-story); [EzUGC blur](https://www.ezugc.ai/blog/instagram-story-blurry-when-i-add-music)
- On a photo Story, a music sticker makes the frame play for the clip's length instead of 5 s. — [Elise Darma / search summary](https://elisedarma.com/blog/instagram-stories-analytics)
- Story reach baseline: ~2 % of followers for large accounts, 6–20 % for small/mid accounts depending on slide position (Socialinsider, May 2026, cited second-hand). — [EmbedSocial](https://embedsocial.com/blog/instagram-stories-analytics/)
- The API cannot add a music sticker to a Story at all. — [Ayrshare](https://www.ayrshare.com/docs/apis/post/social-networks/instagram)

### Inferences
- For MSP's animated Stories (rendered MP4 with their own audio), baking audio in is the only automatable route, and there is no evidence the sticker itself changes reach. Note the existing MSP finding that text added in the app's caption field did not show; any music credit would need to be in-frame.

### Gaps
- No Instagram statement, platform study or controlled experiment on music stickers and Story reach.
