need a professional to confirm before launch (list in section 6).

Labels: **[E]** evidence (official text, law, or a dated professional article; URL and date in Sources),
**[V]** vendor or blog claim, **[I]** our own inference.

## Takeaway: what we must do

1. **Publish official rules** (a public page, e.g. on myspeedpuzzling.com) and link them from every quiz post,
   the bio and the first Story. Instagram requires official rules, eligibility terms, **a complete release of
   Instagram by each entrant**, and a statement that **the promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by or
   associated with Instagram** [E: Instagram Promotion Guidelines]. Czech practice is that the rules are public
   for the whole campaign and any change is announced [E: epravo 2017].
2. **Keep entry free and do not ask for engagement.** With no stake (no purchase, no paid SMS), a free prize draw
   is **not a gambling game** under Czech Act **186/2016** (the gambling act; 187/2016 is the gambling *tax* act):
   gambling needs both a stake *and* chance, § 3(1) [E]. Do **not** make following, liking, sharing or tagging a
   condition: Meta's Spam policy bans giveaways of "cash or cash equivalents in exchange for engagement" [E], and a
   voucher could be read as a cash equivalent [I]. Answering the quiz is the only entry action.
3. **Say exactly how the winner is picked.** Under the Czech Civil Code a public promise of a reward goes by
   default to **whoever performs first** (§ 2886) unless the promise says otherwise [E]. Our rules must state the
   random draw among correct answers, who judges answers and by which measure (§ 2889) [E], and the tie and redraw
   rules. Publish the seed commitment before the week's first quiz.
4. **Give the prizes exactly as described.** Advertising a prize contest and not awarding the prizes, or giving a
   lesser one, is a blacklisted misleading practice (UCPD Annex I point 19; Czech Act 634/1992 Annex 1 letter s) [E].
   Claiming a prize must cost the winner nothing (UCPD point 31; Czech Annex 2 letter h) [E]. So: digital code, no
   shipping fees, no paid membership needed to redeem.
5. **Eligibility: 18+ (recommended) or 15+**, with exclusions for the MSP team, the partner's staff and their
   households. **Exclude Italy and Brazil** (local prize-promotion permits apply) and comprehensively sanctioned
   countries. Add "void where prohibited" [I]. The US and Québec are not a problem at our prize size (section 2.5).
6. **GDPR:** legal basis = **performance of the contest terms (Art. 6(1)(b))** for running it, plus **legitimate
   interest (Art. 6(1)(f))** for publishing the winner's username and the audit log [E: dpopro, I]. Store only
   username, IG-scoped user ID, answer, timestamp and correctness. Delete non-winner entries **60 days after the last
   draw** [I]. Publish a short Art. 13 notice inside the rules.
7. **Tax for winners: nothing to do at our prize size.** Since 2024, prizes from advertising and consumer contests
   are tax-exempt in CZ **up to CZK 50,000 per prize** (§ 10(3)(a) Income Tax Act), not CZK 10,000 as many blogs
   still say. Above that, the organiser withholds 15 % of the **whole** prize (§ 36(2)(i)) [E: law text, KPMG 2025].
   Keep each prize well under CZK 50,000.
8. **Vouchers:** an **MSP membership voucher** sent worldwide is most likely a multi-purpose voucher if we are
   VAT-registered; a **shop's money-value voucher** is usually multi-purpose too (no VAT until redeemed). The
   accountant decides how we book either one (section 4) [E: VAT Act § 15–15b, I].
9. **Partner-supplied prize = branded content.** If a shop or brand gives the voucher in exchange for exposure, the
   posts must use Instagram's **paid partnership label** and tag the partner (with their approval) [E: Meta Branded
   Content Policies]. Write a short partner agreement: who is the legal organiser, who delivers the code, and that
   the codes work abroad.
10. **Make it auditable and private:** publish the seed commitment, the salted-hash entry list, the method and the
   result. Never publish raw usernames of non-winners [I].

## 1. Meta / Instagram rules

### 1.1 Instagram Promotion Guidelines: current text [E]

Source: `help.instagram.com/179379842258600`, page title "Promotion Guidelines | Instagram Help Center", fetched
2026-10-17 (server-rendered text). Verbatim:

> If you use Instagram to communicate or manage a commercial promotion in Brazil, make sure you comply with the
> regulation and are in accordance with the supervision of activities related to the free distribution of prizes
> from the Secretariat of Prizes and Bets - SPA, in the Ministry of Finance.
>
> **Promotions**
> 1. If you use Instagram to communicate or administer a promotion (example: a contest or sweepstakes, among
>    others), you are responsible for the lawful operation of that promotion, including:
>    - The official rules;
>    - Offer terms and eligibility requirements (example: age and residency restrictions, among others); and
>    - Compliance with applicable rules and regulations governing the promotion and all prizes offered (example:
>      registration and obtaining necessary regulatory approvals, among others)
> 2. You must not inaccurately tag content or encourage users to inaccurately tag content (example: don't
>    encourage people to tag themselves in photos if they aren't in the photo).
> 3. Promotions on Instagram must include the following:
>    - A complete release of Instagram by each entrant or participant.
>    - Acknowledgement that the promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with,
>      Instagram.
> 4. We will not assist you in the administration of your promotion and cannot advise you on whether consent is
>    required for use of user content or on how to obtain any necessary consent.
> 5. You agree that if you use our service to administer your promotion, you do so at your own risk.

- The Instagram text does **not** forbid asking people to tag friends. ContestQueen (22 Oct 2024) notes that the
  older "don't ask entrants to tag friends" clause was removed [E]. Tagging friends who aren't in the content is
  still "inaccurate tagging" in spirit, and it is engagement bait [I]. We don't use tags at all.
- Instagram doesn't say where the rules must sit [V: Woobox]. A link in the caption, bio and Story is standard [I].

### 1.2 Facebook / Meta Pages promotion rules [E]

Meta's Pages, Groups and Events policies (Promotions, item 7; read in Czech, 2026-10-17) say: comply with law;
require participants to fully release Meta and acknowledge non-affiliation; and the promotion **must not require
or incentivise participants to share, repost, tag others or otherwise publicise** it. This applies to Facebook
Pages, not Instagram, but it matters if we cross-post [I].

### 1.3 Requiring follows, likes or shares [E/V/I]

- **Meta Community Standards, Spam** (change log up to 26 Jun 2024; fetched 2026-10-17) prohibits "selling, buying,
  or exchanging for engagement, such as likes, shares, views, follows, clicks, use of specific hashtags", including
  "**Offering giveaways (i.e., offering others a chance to win) of cash or cash equivalents in exchange for
  engagement**" (e.g. "Anyone that likes my page will be entered to win $500") [E].
- A shop voucher with a money value is arguably a cash equivalent [I]. "Follow + like to enter" is therefore risky.
  "Answer the quiz to enter" is a knowledge action, not bought engagement [I].
- Blogs disagree on follow-to-enter (Stampede: not allowed; TryUnlockDM 2026: allowed) [V]. Our safe line: **never
  a condition.** We may say "follow so you don't miss tomorrow's quiz" as an invitation [I].
- Facebook has demoted "engagement bait" since 2017 (vote, react, share, tag and comment baiting; "comment with
  specific answers" is listed) [E: Meta Business Help]. That is one more reason to collect answers by **Story reply /
  DM** rather than comments; keep comments as the fallback [I].

### 1.4 Recommendations (reach to non-followers) [E/I]

- The current **Instagram Recommendations Guidelines** (`help.instagram.com/313829416281232`, fetched 2026-10-17)
  list three non-recommendable categories: safety-sensitive content, **low-quality content** (incl. "Long captions
  unrelated to the underlying content and coordinated comment networks intended to artificially drive engagement and
  distribution"), and false or misleading content. **Contests and giveaways are not named** in today's text [E].
- So "giveaway content is never recommended to non-followers" is not in the current official text. Treat it as
  folklore [I]. The real risks are engagement-bait signals (comment-to-win, tag-a-friend) and spammy captions [I].
- Practical: keep quiz posts as real content (a puzzle image, an MSP fact), short captions, and a link to the rules.
  Collect answers by DM. Check "Account Status → recommendations eligibility" in the app during the campaign [V].

### 1.5 Branded content (partner prize) [E]

If a shop or brand supplies the voucher in exchange for being featured, the post is branded content. It "may only
be posted with the use of the branded content tool", which tags the partner with their permission. The Czech
Advertising Act also requires ads to be recognisable [I]. If MSP buys the voucher itself, no label is needed, but
naming the shop is still a mention to handle fairly [I].

## 2. EU and Czech law on prize contests

### 2.1 Not gambling (Act 186/2016) [E]

- **Correction to the brief:** the gambling act is **Act 186/2016 Sb., o hazardních hrách**. 187/2016 is the
  gambling *tax* act.
- § 3(1): a gambling game is "hra, sázka nebo los, do nichž sázející vloží sázku, jejíž návratnost se nezaručuje,
  a v nichž o výhře nebo prohře rozhoduje zcela nebo zčásti náhoda nebo neznámá okolnost" [E, consolidated text
  1 Oct 2025]. Both elements are needed: a **stake** and **chance**.
- Since 1 Jan 2017 consumer contests left the old lottery act. A contest without a stake (or without chance) is
  outside the gambling act. No licence, notification or prize cap (the old CZK 20,000 / 200,000 limits were
  scrapped) [E: epravo/CHSH 15 Feb 2017; V: Nilia, ECOVIS].
- A "stake" can hide in an overpriced product or a premium SMS [E: epravo 2017]. Our entry is a free DM: no stake [I].
- **Discard:** ARROWS (28 Apr 2026, upd. 7 Aug 2026) claims that "§ 3 odst. 2 písm. g)" excludes price contests with
  a CZK 1,000 cap. In the actual law, § 3(2)(g) is "tombola" and § 3 has no such exclusion [E: zakonyprolidi]. The
  claim is unreliable; don't use it.
- "Skill + random draw among correct answers" is fine: chance is present, but there is no stake [I]. Lawyer to
  confirm in one line.

### 2.2 Consumer protection (UCPD 2005/29/EC, Act 634/1992) [E]

- Act 634/1992 § 2(1)(u) defines "spotřebitelská soutěž" as a contest, poll or other prize event for consumers in
  direct connection with promoting the trader's product or service, with winners picked at random, where entry
  depends on a purchase **or "účast spotřebitele na marketingové akci prodávajícího"** (taking part in the trader's
  marketing event) [E]. Our quiz likely fits, so consumer-protection rules apply and ČOI is the regulator [I].
- Blacklist items that matter: UCPD Annex I **pt 19** / CZ Annex 1 **(s)**: offering a prize contest without awarding
  the described prizes or a reasonable equivalent. UCPD **pt 31** / CZ Annex 2 **(h)**: creating a false impression of
  a prize, or making claiming it cost money [E].
- General rule: the practice must meet professional diligence and must not mislead [E: § 4–5 via epravo]. Rules
  should cover entry conditions, how winners are determined, prize description, exclusion grounds and data
  protection, stay published for the whole contest, and changes must be announced [E: epravo 2017]. ČOI fines can
  reach CZK 5 million [V: epravo 2017; turnover-based caps since the Omnibus changes, lawyer to confirm].
- Language: Czech consumers expect Czech information. Publish the rules in **English and Czech**, with the English
  version prevailing for non-Czech entrants. Lawyer to confirm which version should prevail [I].

### 2.3 Civil Code: the rules are a public promise [E]

- §§ 2884–2889 (veřejný příslib, vypsání ceny): a publicly announced promise of a reward binds the promisor.
  Revoking it later doesn't bind someone who already performed in good faith (§ 2885). The reward goes to the
  **first** performer unless the promise says otherwise (§ 2886). If the promise doesn't name who judges and by what
  measure, the promisor judges (§ 2889) [E].
- For us: say "random draw among eligible entrants" explicitly, name MSP as judge, publish the matching rules, and
  reserve changes only for serious reasons, with notice [I].

### 2.4 Age and exclusions [E/I]

- Instagram's minimum age is 13 [E]. The Czech digital-consent age is 15 (Act 110/2019 § 7, GDPR Art. 8) [E], but
  that only matters for consent-based processing. We rely on Art. 6(1)(b) [I].
- A minor's capacity to accept a small prize is fine under Czech law (Civil Code § 31: acts appropriate to their
  maturity) [I]. But winners abroad, voucher terms (shops often require 18+) and contact with minors by DM all add
  risk [I].
- **Recommendation: 18+.** Alternative: 15+, with a parent or guardian confirming before a winner under 18 gets the
  prize. That widens the audience a little and adds one manual step [I]. **Owner decision.**
- Exclude the MSP team, the prize partner's staff, anyone involved in making the quizzes, and their households [I].

### 2.5 International entrants [V/E/I]

| Place | Rule | Our risk at a small prize | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Italy** | DPR 430/2001: random-winner "concorsi a premio" need a ministry notice, a deposit and a notary/official at the draw; foreign organisers must comply [V: agency FAQ, Max Marketing, Leevia] | Real, even for small prizes (>€1 per a 2026 guide [V]) | **Exclude** |
| **Brazil** | Lei 5.768/1971: prize draws need prior SPA/MF authorisation; only Brazilian companies can apply; Instagram's own guidelines flag Brazil [E/V] | Real | **Exclude** |
| **USA** | NY (GBL § 369-e) and FL (§ 849.094): registration and bond only if total prizes exceed USD 5,000; RI over USD 500, mainly retail [V] | None at our size | Say "No purchase necessary", give the prize's approximate value and the odds wording |
| **Québec** | Publicity-contest regime repealed 27 Oct 2023 [E: Canadian Lawyer, Cassels] | None | No exclusion |
| Sanctioned countries | We can't deliver prizes or pay there [I] | Compliance | Exclude "countries under comprehensive EU sanctions" |
| Anywhere else | Local promotion law could apply [I] | Low for a free, small, skill-based draw | "Void where prohibited or restricted by law" |

Mitigations: keep the total prize small (4 × a voucher, roughly CZK 500–2,000 each); use digital codes that work
abroad; say "void where prohibited"; exclude Italy and Brazil; ask the winner's country before handing out the
code [I].

## 3. Data protection (GDPR)

### 3.1 Roles and legal basis

- MSP (the operator; legal entity to fill in) is the **controller**. Meta processes the DMs as an independent
  controller on its platform. A partner shop receiving the winner's name would be a separate controller, so send it
  as little as possible [I].
- **Running the contest** (receiving answers, judging, drawing, contacting the winner, delivering the prize): **Art.
  6(1)(b)**, performance of the contest terms the entrant accepts [V: dpopro.cz; real rules e.g. Lactalis 2026].
  EDPB 2/2019: only what is objectively necessary for that purpose; no bundling of marketing [E].
- **Publishing the winner's username** and the pseudonymised audit log: **Art. 6(1)(f)**, legitimate interest in
  transparency and proof the draw was fair. It is stated in the rules, and the winner can ask to be shown only as
  "winner @a***" [I]. Czech practice varies (consent, contract or legitimate interest) [V]. **Avoid consent** as the
  basis, because it can be withdrawn and Art. 8 then brings in the age-15 issue [I].
- **Marketing** (e.g. a newsletter): never bundled with entry. Ask separately, if at all [E: EDPB 2/2019].

### 3.2 What to store (data minimisation) [I]

| Field | Why | Keep until |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram username | Identify, announce the winner | Non-winners: final draw + 60 days |
| Instagram-scoped user ID (IGSID from the API) | Stable identity; catch duplicates; first answer counts | Same |
| Answer text (normalised and raw) | Judging, disputes | Same |
| Timestamp (UTC) | Deadline, "first answer counts" | Same |
| Correct (yes/no) + rule version | Audit | Same |
| Weekly ticket list (salted hashes) and seed | Public audit log | Can stay public (pseudonymous) |
| Winner: username, prize, code, sent date, claim message | Proof of delivery, accounting | 5 years from the end of the accounting year (Act 563/1991 § 31) if part of an accounting record; otherwise final draw + 12 months [I] |

- Don't store profile photos, follower counts or other DM content. Delete DMs that aren't answers from the dataset
  (they stay in the Instagram inbox, which Meta controls) [I].
- Storage: local on the Mac in CZ (EU), encrypted disk, outside the public repo (`content/`/`var/` are gitignored).
  No third-country transfer by us. Meta's own transfers are Meta's responsibility [I].
- Meta Platform Terms (for API-sourced data) also require deleting data when it is no longer needed, and limit
  use to the declared purpose. Developer docs to check [I].

### 3.3 Art. 13 notice: content checklist [E: GDPR Art. 13; I]

Controller identity and contact; purposes and legal bases (6(1)(b) running the contest; 6(1)(f) announcement and
audit log); the legitimate interest; recipients (Meta as the platform; prize partner only for the winner, if needed;
accountant); no transfers by us outside the EEA; retention periods; rights (access, rectification, erasure,
restriction, objection to 6(1)(f), portability for 6(1)(b)); the right to complain to **ÚOOÚ** (uoou.gov.cz);
that the data is needed to take part (without it no entry); no automated decisions with legal effect (the draw is
random and has no legal or similarly significant effect; mention it for transparency).

### 3.4 Publishing an audit log without exposing people [I]

- Publish per week: the rules version, number of correct entrants, list of **HMAC-SHA256(salt_week, IGSID)**
  values, the seed commitment and the reveal, and the winning index.
- Unsalted hashes of usernames can be reversed by guessing usernames, so they are still personal data. Use the
  numeric IGSID with a secret salt. Reveal the salt only to a participant who asks (so they can check their own
  entry), or publish it after the claim period. Trade-off: publishing the salt lets anyone test whether a known
  person entered (low harm, but say so in the notice).
- Children: with 18+ there is no children's data by design. If someone discloses they are under age, delete the
  entry [I].

## 4. Prizes and vouchers

### 4.1 Income tax for winners [E]

- § 10(1)(ch) ZDP: "výhry z reklamních soutěží a reklamních slosování, ceny z veřejných soutěží…" are "other
  income". § 10(3)(a), as amended from 2024: income subject to withholding "je od daně osvobozen, pokud výše tohoto
  příjmu nepřesahuje 50000 Kč". § 36(2)(i): 15 % withholding on these prizes [E: zakonyprolidi, consolidated
  1 Aug 2026].
- EY (23 Apr 2025, citing Finanční správa): from 2024 the limit is per prize; above CZK 50,000 the operator withholds
  15 %. KPMG/Daňovky (31 Mar 2025): exempt up to CZK 50,000 per prize; above that, **the whole prize** is taxed at
  15 % [E]. Finzi.cz "2026" still says CZK 10,000, which is out of date [V].
- Non-residents: Czech-source contest prizes are Czech-taxable (§ 22(1)(g)(8)) [E]. Usually the winner's country
  taxes them too, under the treaty [E: KPMG]. At CZK ≤ 2,000 there is no Czech withholding (accountant to confirm
  that § 10(3)(a) applies to non-residents). Any tax in the winner's own country is up to the winner (say so in the
  rules) [I].
- Prizes linked to the winner's business or employment don't get the exemption [E: KPMG]. That is one more reason
  to exclude the team.

### 4.2 VAT on vouchers [E/I]

Czech VAT Act §§ 15–15b implement Directive 2016/1065 [E]:
- **Single-purpose voucher (SPV)**: when issued, the place of supply and the VAT rate are known. Transferring it
  counts as the supply itself (§ 15a) [E].
- **Multi-purpose voucher (MPV)**: any other voucher. Transfer is not a supply. VAT arises on redemption (§ 15b) [E].
- **MSP membership voucher (own service):** if MSP is a VAT payer, a membership voucher for an electronically
  supplied service is SPV only if the rate and place are known when issued. With winners worldwide (B2C e-services
  are taxed where the customer lives), the place isn't known in advance, so it is likely **MPV** [I]. Giving away
  our own service free for business (marketing) purposes is generally **not** a deemed supply: § 14(3)(a) taxes
  free services only when supplied "pro účely nesouvisející s uskutečňováním ekonomické činnosti plátce" (for
  purposes unrelated to the payer's economic activity) [E text; I application]. If MSP is **not** VAT-registered,
  VAT is irrelevant. **Accountant.**
- **Partner shop voucher**: if MSP buys a money-value gift card from a shop, it is usually an MPV with no VAT on the
  purchase; it is a marketing cost [I]. If the partner gives it for free, the partner books it, and MSP may owe
  exposure (branded content) [I]. Gifts of *goods* are a deemed supply unless the cost is ≤ CZK 500 excl. VAT
  (§ 13(7)(c)) [E]. Vouchers aren't goods, but the accountant should confirm.
- Booking: prize cost = advertising/promotion expense, tax-deductible if it serves to earn taxable income.
  Refreshments are not deductible [E: KPMG 2025]. Keep the invoice and a handover record (winner username, code ID,
  date) [I].

### 4.3 Delivery, claim, redraw, announcement [I]

- **Delivery:** digital code by Instagram DM (or e-mail if the winner prefers). Check before the campaign that the
  code works internationally and that shipping, minimum spend and currency rules are fair. List these in the rules
  (UCPD pt 31).
- **Notification:** DM from @myspeedpuzzling within 24 h of the draw. Warn in every Story: "We will never ask for
  passwords, payment or card details". Impersonator accounts are common in giveaways.
- **Claim deadline: 7 days** from notification. If there's no reply, an ineligible winner, or the prize is refused:
  **redraw** with the same seed, taking the next index of the same shuffled list (deterministic). Maximum two
  redraws, then the prize rolls into the next week or goes back to MSP (say which in the rules).
- **Announcement:** a Story on the draw day: username of the winner (shown as `@name`, or masked on request),
  number of eligible entrants, and a link to the audit log.
- **Week 4 timing:** 24–26 Dec are holidays (Štědrý den, 1st and 2nd Christmas Day). Quizzes can be pre-scheduled,
  but answer judging and the 27 Dec draw need someone on duty. The 7-day claim window then runs into the New Year
  [I].
