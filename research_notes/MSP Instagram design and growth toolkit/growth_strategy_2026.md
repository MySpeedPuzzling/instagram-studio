## 1. Posting frequency

### 1.1 What the evidence says

| Source (sample, date) | Finding | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Buffer, 2.1M feed posts, 102k accounts, published 13 Aug 2025 (no 2026 re-run) | Within-account models: weekly follower growth +0.12 % (1–2 posts/wk), +0.26 % (3–5), +0.44 % (6–9), +0.66 % (10+). **Median reach per post goes up, not down**, with frequency: +12 % / +18 % / +24 % vs 1–2/wk. Zero-post weeks fall below the account's own baseline. No breakdown by account size; Stories excluded; "multiple per day" not analysed | [E] |
| Dash Social, 1.5M feed posts + 1.1M Stories, 3,000+ brands, Jul 2025–Jun 2026 | Highest **engagement rate** at 2–3 feed posts/week; Reels best at 1–3/week; Stories best completion rate at **30+/week** (~4/day). Large brands; ER not reach | [E] |
| Quid (Rival IQ) 2026 benchmark, 150 companies per industry | Median brand posts **3.69/week** on Instagram (lowest in six years); median ER 0.30 % by followers | [E] |
| Hootsuite posting-schedule guide, 2026 | 3–5 feed posts/week, Stories 2×/day minimum; avg business frequency 4.5/week (Q4 2024) | [E] (expert guidance on own data) |
| SocialTools AI, 2,400 accounts, ~May 2026 | Per-post ER falls ~4× from < 1 post/wk to 7–14/wk, while **total weekly engagement rises 5.4×**; confounded by size (bigger accounts post more) | [E] weak |
| Photoslurp via Social Media Today, 476 fashion brands, 7 Jun 2018 | Engagement per post −19 % going from 0.5 to 1.5 posts/day, −9 % more to 2.5; total daily engagement 2.2× at 3/day vs 1/day | [E] old, large brands |
| Mosseri via RouteNote, 6 Dec 2024 | Some successful creators post once a month, others five Reels a day; "in general, the more you post, the more reach you'll get", pick a schedule you can sustain | [E] (secondary report of his Reel) |
| Mosseri, 7 Mar 2025 (via InstantDM) | Posting another Reel while one is taking off "should not affect that reach", especially if it doesn't go up immediately after; "there's no perfect number" | [E] secondary |
| Mosseri, Sep 2026 ranking series (Social Media Today, 24 Sep 2026) | Ranking predicts how each person will respond; **recency matters a lot**; small creators' public posts are tested on non-followers; focus on **engagement rate, not total views**; post as much as you can without burning out; use Trial Reels to experiment | [E] |
| Mosseri, Apr 2024 Threads (via InstantDM) | Nobody reaches all followers: many don't open the app that day, and those who do have more posts than time | [E] secondary |

**Reading [O]:** two kinds of evidence agree once you separate them. Per-post *rates* (ER) fall as you post more; per-post
*reach* and *total* reach and followers rise, with diminishing returns. The rate drop is partly the account becoming
bigger and partly the same followers seeing more posts. For MSP, whose regular post already reaches ~37 % of followers
(~14× the small-account benchmark), the risk is real: our followers are the ones who'd see a second post the same day.

### 1.2 MSP's own evidence [M]

- The account has **never sustained 3+ posts/week** in the insights era: since 2025, 27 weeks had 1 post, 11 had 2,
  3 had 3. Week-level reach shows nothing yet (n too small). Every cadence above 2/week is untested for MSP.
- Three same-day pairs (Jan, Feb, Jun 2025): combined reach 1,921 / 1,896 / 1,460 (median single post ~900). The weaker
  post reached 88 %, 54 % and 86 % of its sibling; the one at 54 % was an early-morning image vs an evening carousel.
  Anecdote, but consistent with "more total, each a little less".
- Dormant months reach ~50 accounts; active months 1–3k; the WJPC month 5k (audit). Consistency is the biggest lever we
  already have evidence for.
- Logged results: Sat 17.7 %, Sun 18.9 % (≈ 1.4× a weekday); on weekends only 31–32 % are logged 18–23 h vs 33–36 % on
  weekdays, so weekend solving runs through the day (`queries/activity-weekday.sql`, 8 weeks to 2026-10-12).

### 1.3 Verdict on frequency and multiple posts per day [O]

- **4/week is the right start**: inside the evidence-backed 3–5 band, above the industry median, and the owner's own
  posts come on top. 5/week is the target once quality holds; it adds one more "at-bat" a week for a ~+6 pp reach
  gain per Buffer, and the bottleneck is approval and idea quality, not the algorithm.
- **Two feed posts in a day: only with a reason.** OK when (a) it's an **event day** (championship result, record, a
  milestone crossing) where recency matters and the second post is a different thing; (b) the second is a **Reel** (a
  different surface: the Reels tab and non-followers) and goes up ≥ 4–6 h after the first; (c) a Trial Reel, which never
  reaches followers' feeds, so it doesn't count. Not OK: two carousels the same day, or a second post to "use up" a
  queue. Owner's manual posts count toward the day.
- **Stories:** 1–3 frames on 4–6 days a week, more on event days (5–10). Mosseri said in Sep 2025 that posting many
  Stories in a day no longer hurts their reach ("not at all the intended behavior") [E: Engadget, 12 Sep 2025]; resharing
  our own feed post to Stories "is not going to meaningfully change your reach" [E: SMT, 6 Apr 2026], so Stories should
  carry *their own* content (poll, quiz, the answer, behind-the-scenes), with a feed reshare only as a pointer.

## 2. Format mix for growth in 2026

### 2.1 Reach vs engagement by format (small accounts where available)

| Source | Reels | Carousels | Images | Note |

| Socialinsider benchmarks 2025 → Q2 2026, ER by followers | 0.52 → 0.48 % | 0.55 → **0.50 %** | 0.37 → 0.33 % | Views/post at 1–5k: carousel 993, Reels 580, image 417 (round 1) |
| Buffer, 45M+ posts (format study) | reach 1.36× carousels, 2.25× images | **ER/reach 6.90 %** | 4.44 % | Reels ER/reach 3.31 %; reach part from an older 2022–24 subset |
| Metricool, 24.4M posts, Jan–Feb 2026 vs 2025 (16 Jun 2026) | +29 % reach vs carousels, 4× image interactions, 8.5 s avg watch | +20 % total views vs Reels, 9× image saves; 76 % of views in 72 h | reach −22 % YoY | |

**Reading [O]:** carousels are the best "followers + saves + sends" format and MSP's natural data container; Reels are
the best ticket to non-followers at our size, with a high skip rate, so they need a real hook. Single images are
declining everywhere except as big announcements (MSP's feature drops did well as images: median reach 1,033).

### 2.2 Ranking signals that matter for the mix

- Mosseri, May 2026: "like rates matter more for your followers… send rates matter more for those who don't follow
  you"; engagement includes likes, sends, comments, saves "et cetera"; focus on the rate among people who saw it, not
  reach [E: SMT, 26 May 2026]. Watch time leads for Reels (round 1).
- Comments and "two-way conversation" as named signals appear only in marketing blogs (2026), no Mosseri quote found
  [E: unverified]. Treat comments as the goal of community posts and as a conversation driver, not as a proven ranking
  lever. Replying to comments correlates with +21 % engagement in Buffer's data [E].
- Originality: since 30 Apr 2026, reposting accounts lose recommendations; Sep 2026 Mosseri restated that the original
  creator gets the distribution [E]. MSP's own data graphics are original by construction; avoid screenshots of others' posts.
- AI content is labelled, not down-ranked (Mosseri, Jul 2026: "I don't think we should filter out AI content") [E]; the
  community-sentiment risk from round 1 still governs (decisions.md).

### 2.3 Trial Reels (2026 status)

- Eligibility per Help Center versions of May and Sep 2026: public account, **≥ 200 followers on a professional
  account** (1,000 personal), and the account must be eligible for recommendations [E: Creator Tribune, CreatorFlow,
  secondary]. MSP (2,405, business) qualifies; confirm the toggle in the app.
- Scheduling of Trial Reels since early 2026; Meta says creators using them saw +80 % non-follower Reels reach and 40 %
  then posted Reels more often [E: SMT, Apr 2026, company figures].
- **A trial "may get limited reach" if Instagram detects content shared before** [E: secondary on Help Center] → hook
  variants must differ visibly (different first 3 s, cover, text), not be re-uploads.
- Mosseri recommends Trial Reels for experimenting (Sep 2026) [E]. They don't touch followers' feeds, so they sit
  outside the weekly count and outside the fatigue caps.

### 2.4 Collabs

- Up to **5 collaborators** per feed post/Reel, and the original author can add collaborators after publishing
  (Help Center wording quoted by third-party guides, Oct 2026) [E: secondary]. Not on Stories. The API limit used by
  our publisher is in the round-2 report.
- Collab reach is a weighted average that favours the smaller partner; Emplifi H1 2026 shows collabs lifting shares in
  every vertical [E: round 2]. → MSP benefits most by collaborating **upward**: organisers on event posts, brands on
  their puzzle's spotlight, the largest featured player on a record post.

**What measurably builds followers [O from E+M]:** non-follower reach × a reason to follow. Non-follower reach comes from
sends, Reels/Trial Reels, collabs, events and tagged people resharing; the reason to follow comes from a recognisable
series and a profile grid that promises more of the same value. Likes alone (follower-only content) keep the base warm but
don't grow it. So every week needs at least one post built for **sends** (benchmark, myth-buster, identity fact) and one
built for **comments** (community), and the profile grid must read as "the numbers of speed puzzling".

## 4. Added value, not slop: the quality bar

### 4.1 Principles

1. **Something only MSP can make** [E: Mosseri's "something that only you could create", originality ranking]: the
   data behind every post comes from MSP and isn't available elsewhere.
2. **One clear takeaway per post** [E: OWID "a clear chart and takeaway"]; number first, then what it means for a normal
   puzzler (standing feedback).
3. **Useful, surprising or about me** [E: Berger & Milkman: surprise, interest, practical utility; identity/comparison
   from Wrapped]: a reader must be able to say what they got.
4. **Repeat the container, never the content** [E: Mosseri on repeatable patterns; M: recap decay]: series are fine;
   the same fact, chart grammar or protagonist twice in a row is not.
5. **Honest numbers** [O, required by CLAUDE.md]: fresh data, scope and date stated, exclusions applied, no rounding
   that changes the story.

### 4.2 The six gates (every draft, before it reaches the owner)

| # | Gate | Pass when | Typical fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **One new thing** | The reader learns a fact they didn't know *or* gets a benchmark/tool for their own puzzling *or* sees someone recognised *or* laughs at something true about puzzling | "Here are some numbers from this week" with no headline |
| G2 | **Number → meaning** | Slide 1 headline is a takeaway, not a label; the caption's first line says what it means for a puzzler | Headline "Weekly stats #12" |
| G3 | **Visual carries the number** | Every key number sits on a chart, board, box image or piece art (standing feedback); slide 2 stands alone | Text-only slides; a chart that needs the caption |
| G4 | **A reason to send or answer** | Names its mechanic (surprise, identity, comparison, utility, debate, send-a-friend, collective milestone, humour; round 2) and its primary metric; the ask is specific and earnest | Generic "Thoughts?"; "tag 3 friends"; "comment YES" |
| G5 | **Honest data** | Queried `--fresh` < 24 h, rules applied (suspicious, private, opt-outs, do-not-feature), scope + date on the source line, numbers re-checked against facts.json | Stale cache, "all-time" that is really "last 8 weeks" |
| G6 | **Not a repeat** | Different from the last 2 posts in chart grammar and protagonist; topic not used in the last ~6 weeks unless it's a series slot; caps from round 2 hold | Third bar chart of the week; same brand as Monday |

A draft that fails a gate is reworked or dropped; it never goes to the owner "to see". The `ig-draft` skill should
print the six gates with a one-line justification each in the draft summary [O].

### 4.3 Red flags (slop signals)

- Filler posts to keep a cadence (a "happy Friday" image, a stat without a takeaway). A missed slot beats a weak post;
  use the evergreen reserve instead.
- AI-looking art (standing feedback), stock-like imagery, generic captions with AI tells (round-2 lint).
- Engagement bait, contest mechanics outside the ≤ 3/year budget, polls with no real question.
- Same template in consecutive posts; captions with the same opening pattern three posts in a row.
- Numbers without a denominator or comparison ("12,000 results" — vs what?).
- Ops/corrections on the feed (move to Stories; round 1).

### 4.4 How data posts stay interesting week after week [O, using round-2 levers]

- **Rotate the protagonist:** puzzle, brand, piece count, country, player (good news only), the whole community, time
  of day, "one year ago".
- **Rotate the question:** guess, rank, spot the outlier, this-or-that, where do you land, myth or true.
- **Rotate the chart grammar:** board, ranked bars, dot plot, distribution, tile map, timeline, waffle of pieces, box wall.
- **Change the time frame:** week, month, season, all-time, an event, a holiday.
- **Use the community's own questions:** comments and Story question-box answers become next week's data story
  (the cheapest source of "things people want to know").
- **Weekly Numbers keeps 3 fixed slides + 2 rotating segments** and is retooled between seasons (round 2).

### 5.1 Metrics per post type (judged at +7 d, against MSP's rolling 12-post median of the same type)

|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly Numbers (recap) | sends/reach | likes/reach, tagged-player reposts | comments (not its job) |

| Trial Reel | 72-h views and sends among non-followers | watch time; whether it auto-shared | likes/reach (audience is all non-followers) |
| Stories | sticker taps / viewers, replies | forward/exit taps (`navigation`), snapshot within 24 h | |
| Account (weekly) | net follows (follows − unfollows) | non-follower share of reach (7-day), website clicks/UTM sessions | |

Per-post counts (saves ~1, follows ~1, shares ~2) are too sparse to judge single posts (round 2); read them per pillar and per month.

### 5.2 Experiment cadence [O, protocol from round 2]

- **One feed test at a time**, pre-registered in the hypothesis log with a fixed n; ABAB on matched slots; judged on
  episodes 3–6 for new formats; event weeks excluded.
- **Trial Reels as the weekly bench:** 1–3 a week once motion builders exist, each a visibly different hook of one data story.
- **Cadence:** a new feed test every ~4 weeks (that's what 4 posts/week can resolve for 2× effects); a monthly review
  with the `ig-insights` skill; learnings go to `editorial/learnings.md` labelled "anecdote" until n is met.

### 5.3 Decision rules for frequency [O]

Compare a 4-week block with the previous 4-week block (excluding event weeks and giveaways):

| Rule | Increase one step (4→5, later 5→6) when ALL hold | Hold when | Step back when ANY holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-post reach | median ≥ 90 % of previous block | 75–90 % | < 75 % for the block |
| Follower resonance | median likes/reach ≥ 90 % | | < 80 % |
| Growth | weekly net follows ≥ previous block | flat | unfollows up ≥ 50 % with no event explaining it |
| Quality | every post passed the 6 gates; no post was "filler" | 1 weak post | ≥ 2 posts the owner called filler/slop |
| Capacity | owner approval fits one sitting a week; ideas bank ≥ 3 weeks ahead | | approvals slip, zero-post risk |

Total reach rising while per-post reach falls 10–25 % is the expected trade-off of more volume (Buffer, Photoslurp) and
is acceptable; a fall beyond 25 % means the extra posts are cannibalising the same followers. Re-evaluate after every
2 blocks; the algorithm and the audience move.

**Baselines to beat** (audit, to be recomputed from `content/state.sqlite` once the pipeline posts): followers 2,405;
median reach per regular post ~900 (2026 so far ~1,200); ER ~8.5 %; shares ~2, saves ~1, follows ~1 per post; net
follows +30–90/month in active non-event months.

### 6.3 Quality checklist to print with every draft

1. **G1 One new thing:** what does the reader get? (fact / benchmark / recognition / a true laugh)
2. **G2 Number → meaning:** slide-1 headline is the takeaway; caption line 1 = number + what it means for a puzzler.
3. **G3 Visual carries the number:** chart/board/box/pieces; slide 2 works alone; H4 house style.
4. **G4 Mechanic + ask:** named mechanic, named primary metric, one specific earnest question or send line.
5. **G5 Honest data:** `--fresh` < 24 h, exclusions applied, do-not-feature checked, scope and date on the source line.
6. **G6 Not a repeat:** chart grammar and protagonist differ from the last 2 posts; caps respected.
7. Caption 300–800 characters, 2–4 hashtags, 0–3 emoji, alt text written; tags only for good news.
8. Slot fits the rhythm (no second post the same day unless the §1.3 exception applies).

## Gaps

- No study separates 1/day from 2/day for small accounts; the multiple-per-day verdict rests on old large-brand data,
